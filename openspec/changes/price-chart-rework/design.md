## Chart rework (4 Nov 2026)

Owner feedback on a phone, decided without the owner per the coordinator. The dataviz skill was read first; where it and the owner differ, the owner's phone wins and the reason is here.

- **Weight.** Line 1.5 dp, markers 3 dp radius with a 1.5 dp surface ring. Dataviz asks for a 2 px line and 4 px markers on the web; 2 dp on a 420 dpi phone is over 5 px and the owner found it heavy. Gridlines are 1 dp in `line`.
- **Crowding.** Markers show only while neighbours average 12 dp apart or more; past that only the latest point keeps one. A bead chain of dots says less than the line.
- **Ranges.** Chips (M3 `FilterChip`, as the item chips above already are: 32 dp to see, 48 dp to touch) above the plot, as dataviz puts filters above what they scope. 6M, 1Y, 3Y, All, counted back from the latest point, not today: an item last bought a year ago still shows its last year of prices. A range is offered only when it holds two points and fewer than the next longer range, so a short history shows no chips, and 6M is left out when it shows what 1Y shows (seen on the emulator: rows yearly since 2016 and two in late October made 6M and 1Y the same two points). Rejected: disabled chips, which show options that do nothing. Rejected: `SegmentedTabs`, a full-width bar right above the Daily/Monthly/Yearly bar reads as a second set of tabs. Opens on 1Y when offered, else All. The pick is kept per item with `rememberSaveable` on the screen, like the card's fold.
- **Figures follow the range.** Lowest, highest, change and the header's spoken sentence use the range's points. The latest price is the same in every range.
- **Monthly points.** When the range's points span more than 18 months and number more than 60, one point per month on the 1st, at the average of its days, half up in `Long`. Ten years of weekly shopping is 120 points, not 520. A few trips a year stay as days: on the emulator, 3Y with four rows averaged two October days into one point that matched neither, while the figures below named the real lowest. Lowest and highest always name the real day.
- **X by date** stays, so a gap reads as a gap; the range chips and monthly points are what keep ten years readable.
- **Y axis.** 3 or 4 gridlines: the smallest step of 1, 2, 2,5 or 5 times a power of ten, at least Rp 100, that spans the prices in at most 3 steps, starting at a step at or below the lowest price, not at zero (a price moving between 35 rb and 41 rb is flat on an axis from 0). A flat price gets a band a tenth of it wide either side. Labels use the existing `formatIdrAxis`, now exact rather than rounded (`2,5 rb`, `38,9 rb`, `1,25 jt`): Stats' `2.500` tick used to read `3 rb`. No second formatter.
- **X axis.** First, middle and last dates of the points shown (`28 Jul 2025`, or `Jul 2025` when monthly); the middle one is dropped when it would touch the others.
- **Selection.** Tap or horizontal drag selects the nearest point by date (`nearestPoint`, earlier on a tie). A 1 dp `ink3` guide drops through it; its marker becomes a 4 dp dot in a 7 dp ring; a label in a band above the plot reads the date and the price in `MoneyText`, centred on the point and kept inside the card. With nothing selected the band reads "Tap the line to see a price". Tapping the selected point again, or changing range or item, clears it. The band is always there so the plot does not jump.
- **TalkBack.** The header keeps its one sentence. The chart is one adjustable node: "Price chart, 12 points from 6 Nov 2025 to 30 Oct 2026"; swipe up or down steps through the points, each spoken "28 Jul 2025, Rp 100.000" (or "July 2025, average Rp 100.000"), with Next price and Previous price actions as well. Chips speak "Last 6 months" and so on. The figures stay hidden, as they repeat the sentence.
- **Size and font.** Plot 136 dp (was 88), fixed in dp; axis text is caption capped at 130% like other slot labels, so 200% font keeps the plot's shape.
- **Placement.** The chart moved from `ui/shared/PriceLine.kt` to `ui/search/PriceLineChart.kt`: it now knows `PriceChart`, and search is its only user.
